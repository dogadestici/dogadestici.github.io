Conformal Compactification and Penrose Diagram of the Schwarzschild Spacetime NOVEMBER 2025 - ONGOING
IN PROGRESS

I am actively working on this independent research project, which I initiated during my Erasmus semester in Czechia. Within the scope of this work, I investigate the causal structure of the Schwarzschild black hole spacetime using differential geometric methods, focusing on applying conformal compactification techniques on Lorentzian manifolds to map infinite spacetime boundaries onto a finite Penrose diagram.

Status: Research notes and project outputs will be published here soon.

Note: This study is an independent research project conducted without an academic advisor. Suggestions on literature, methodology, or mathematical insights are always greatly valued.
