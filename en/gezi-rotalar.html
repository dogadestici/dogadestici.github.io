My Travels

My passion for travel began with a journey I took with my mother in 2025, and traveling soon became an essential part of my life. Since then, I have returned to the streets of familiar cities and dreamed of visiting new places. I have collected incredible memories along every route, and here I share these experiences.

BUDAPEST

// Hungary - February, 2025 //

This was my first experience in Budapest, and it is a truly mesmerizing city. Even though I visited in winter, it served as a wonderful destination for a first international trip. It easily ranks among my favorite places.

VIENNA

// Austria - February, 2025 //

Votive Church

Vienna is a beautiful city, though it didn't deeply captivate me on this visit. Nevertheless, it is a city that deserves dedicated time and budget to explore its museums one by one.

BRATISLAVA

// Slovakia - February, 2025 //

Slovak National Theatre

A very small yet charming town.

ROME

// Italy - May, 2025 //

Colosseum

I am not sure if any city can quite surpass Rome. It is filled with history, wonderful food, and welcoming people. It holds a particularly special place in my memory as it was the first time I traveled abroad independently with friends.

POTENZA § PIGNOLA

// Italy - May, 2025 //

Pignola

I visited the Italian village of Pignola for an Erasmus+ project. We had such an incredible time that this happens to be the only photograph I took there. Despite usually preferring larger cities, Pignola's charm made me feel like I could happily stay much longer.

NAPLES

// Italy - May, 2025 //

Piazza Garibaldi

Since we only had a few hours in Naples, I didn't have time for an extensive tour or a proper meal. Having later met many Italian friends from Naples, a return visit is certainly on my list.

PRAGUE

// Czechia - September, 2025 //

My first Prague trip during Erasmus

Prague holds a unique place for me. Exploring the city while experiencing Czech culture and daily life made it a memorable journey.

VIENNA

// Austria - October, 2025 //

Burggarten

This visit was much more enjoyable than my first time in Vienna. Navigating and discovering the city independently made for a much richer experience, showing how revisiting a place can offer new perspectives.

BUDAPEST

// Hungary - October, 2025 //

Buda Castle

My second trip to Budapest was with my Erasmus friends. Combining both visits, I spent about a week in the city; walking around listening to music was a peaceful experience.

PRAGUE

// Czechia - October, 2025 //

Charles Bridge

When my friends visited, I got to guide them around Czechia locally. Having dreamed of being there with them for months, exploring Prague together was a great experience.

PARIS

// France - November, 2025 //

Paris

Paris left a strong impression on me. Despite initial preconceptions, I found it to be a wonderful city and hope to live there for a period in the future.

MUNICH

// Germany - New Year, 2025 //

New Year's Eve

Celebrating New Year's Eve in my friend's Erasmus city was a wonderful experience. Hosting each other in our respective exchange destinations added meaningful value to the journey.

MADRID

// Spain - February, 2026 //

Madrid

I had the opportunity to explore Madrid before an Erasmus+ project. A friend had mentioned that Madrid feels like the Ankara of Spain, and they were right. I thoroughly enjoyed Spain and would love to revisit Madrid in the future.

VIENNA

// Austria - May, 2026 //

Vienna x3

After returning from Erasmus, I traveled back with close friends to revisit my exchange city and reconnect with friends there—a trip that brought great joy.

BRNO

My Brno :')

Returning felt like coming back home. Brno will always hold a special place in my memories. Czechia and Brno... Moc miluju, vždy budu milovat.

PRAGUE

An Evening in Prague

This time it was particularly difficult to leave. A truly special city.

KRAKOW

Krakow - Old Town

Poland conveys its history exceptionally well, and Krakow left a lasting impression in that regard.

WARSAW

Warsaw - Old Town

Warsaw was wonderful. The cuisine and streets were memorable, making it a great place to explore.
