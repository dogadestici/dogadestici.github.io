What Are Erasmus+ Youth Projects & How to Participate?

Although academic semester exchanges are usually the first thing that comes to mind with Erasmus, the European Union's Erasmus+ Youth Exchanges offer fantastic short-term opportunities (1-2 weeks) to gain global perspectives and practical skills.

You do not necessarily need to be enrolled in a university exchange program to participate. Non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and youth associations serve as the primary gateways for involvement:

  • Association Calls: Follow open calls published by accredited youth organizations.
  • Application Forms: Expressing genuine interest and motivation regarding the project topic is key.
  • Grants & Budget: Accommodation, meals, and travel expenses are largely covered by EU grants.
💡 Recommendation: You can learn practical tips about motivation letters, application procedures, and upcoming opportunities by following Alp Şekeryapan’s Instagram account (@alpsekeryapan).

Details regarding the international Erasmus+ projects I have attended—ranging from digital production processes in Pignola, Italy, to environmental responsibility workshops in San Lorenzo de El Escorial, Spain—are outlined below.

Erasmus+ Youth Exchange Program

// Pignola, Italy — May 2024 //

Digital Storytelling, Video Production, and Intercultural Exchange

In this international training program held in Pignola, Italy, I engaged in work focused on digital storytelling and video production. Collaborating with multicultural teams from various European countries, I participated in intercultural workshops centered on media content creation, visual editing, and media literacy.

Erasmus+ Youth Project

// San Lorenzo de El Escorial, Spain — February 2026 //

Sustainability, Environmental Responsibility, and Social Innovation Initiatives

During this international project held in the historic town of San Lorenzo de El Escorial near Madrid, I contributed to initiatives centered on sustainability strategy development, environmental responsibility, and ecological awareness. The program focused on non-formal learning methods and strategic partnerships aimed at fostering environmental consciousness.
