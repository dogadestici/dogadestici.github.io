Ankarock & Metal Music Society

Ankarock Welcome Event - 25.10.2023

Founded at Ankara University, Ankarock is a student organization that approaches rock and metal music not merely as genres, but as a unifying culture. Our goal is to connect listeners who appreciate rock, metal, blues, and jazz both on and off campus. Whether one plays an instrument or not, I contribute to creating an inclusive space for everyone who shares an appreciation for this music by organizing concerts, talks, festival logistics, and listening sessions. We continue to expand Ankarock and keep Ankara's rich rock and metal culture vibrant.

What We Do

Under the Ankarock umbrella, we engage with music and culture collaboratively. Key events we regularly organize to strengthen community ties include:

  • • Welcome Meetings: Gathering at the beginning of academic terms to greet new members, build connections, and foster a welcoming community atmosphere.
  • • Jam Sessions: Interactive sessions where musicians take the stage for improvisational sessions and musical collaboration.
  • • Rock & Metal Talks: Dedicated discussion sessions examining the history, philosophy, albums, and cultural influence of specific bands or movements in rock and metal.

You can follow our upcoming events on social media and participate in ticket giveaways for local concerts in Ankara. Students at Ankara University are welcome to join directly, while non-members can attend all our open events. You can also explore or contribute to our "Ankarock Cult" article series featuring album and artist reviews using the link below.

ESN (Erasmus Student Network)

What is ESN (Erasmus Student Network)?
ESN is one of the largest student organizations in Europe, supporting internationalization in higher education, facilitating intercultural integration, and providing peer-to-peer support for exchange students.

Following my own Erasmus+ exchange, I joined ESN Ankara University to support incoming international students with their integration into the city, local culture, and academic environment. As a candidate member, I assist with organization efforts and peer mentorship.

// DISCOVERING PANOPTIKUM //

12.12.2025 - Namesti Svobody - BRNO

During my Erasmus term in Czechia, live music was one of the things I missed most. In Turkey, I regularly went to concerts by underground and local bands, but there I felt completely disconnected from that scene. While I found venues that fit my taste, I had a limited group of friends and felt hesitant to go alone.

Four months into Erasmus, on a very cold winter day while walking through the Christmas market, my friend and I saw a band doing a soundcheck. We decided to stay and listen, and we absolutely loved their sound. Luckily, they had another concert the following week, which we went to as well. That day made me realize how great it can be to follow something spontaneously, even on your own.

I have been listening to Panoptikum almost every day since. It helped me overcome my hesitancy and added Czech rock to my playlists.
